## Discovered
- STOPPED before code (ground rule: no out-of-scope edits). `types::EndpointPrimitiveState` documents `g0`/`g1` "at Level=1/Level=2" but carries ONE `source_g0`/`source_g1` pair, and `boundary::natural_lower/natural_upper` use it for both the Level-1 part (F1, FN: SOLUTE.FOR:781-789, 869-877) and the Level-2 part (E1/D1/F2, BN/DN/FE: 794-799, 895-901). In the source each MatSet call sees the g0/g1 that its own Coeff level just wrote (112-128), and they differ whenever water, temperature or concentration differ between levels. A single pair is therefore wrong for one of the two levels in general, so the endpoint rows and the F1/FN totals used by the flux closure (800, 902, 204, 221) cannot be exact. `assemble_interior` is fine (per-call g arrays).
- Fix needs an edit to `boundary.rs` and `types.rs` (split into `old_source_g0/g1` and `new_source_g0/g1`, with F1/FN reading the old pair), and the adapter/tests that build the state. Neither file is in 18.10.1 Must edit. A driver-side workaround (two kernel calls plus re-deriving `assembled` and the flux totals) would duplicate kernel logic and is not recommended.
- Minor fidelity note: `transport::coefficients` forms `bulk_density*equilibrium_fraction*...`; the source is `ro*Frac*f_em*xKs*dConc`, so passing `Frac*f_em` as one factor regroups the f32 product when `f_em != 1` (lDualNEq only).
- STOP (transport.rs): `transport::coefficients` takes one `equilibrium_fraction` and multiplies `bulk_density*equilibrium_fraction*sorption_coefficient*concentration_derivative` (and the Disper denominator likewise). It cannot take `Frac` and `f_em` separately, so the source grouping `ro*Frac*f_em*xKs*dConc` cannot be kept for lDualNEq. Per orchestrator instruction, stopped without editing transport.rs.

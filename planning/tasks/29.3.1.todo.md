## Notes
- Entrypoint: `hydrus_core::heat::vapor::driver::VaporWaterHeatDriver::step` (public, used by `vapor_nonuniform_balance.rs`), returns `VaporStepReport` with old/new liquid and vapor contents. No new entrypoint needed, so no STOP.
- Sign: Rust flux is positive into the domain at both ends (design sec. 6, water.rs docs). Lower `flux = rBot`; upper `flux = -rTop`. Net in per time = lower.flux + upper.flux.
- Storage uses trapezoid weights (as `reservoir.domain_storage_rate` in balance.rs) so it matches the half-cell boundary rows.
- Tolerance: the scheme is a linearized-storage Picard solve with water_tolerance, not exact. Proposed: relative 1e-3 of |net_in*dt| for the driven case (plus absolute floor 1e-9), and 1e-9 absolute for control; I will first measure the residual and then state the tolerance in the test with a margin of at least 10x over the measured value, no looser than the above. Risk: if the measured residual exceeds 1e-3 relative, STOP and report (would indicate a real balance defect in the 29.2.x rows, not something to tune away).
- Risks: step may need a small dt / modest fluxes to stay within the retention table; heat with Dirichlet T and uniform T gives near-isothermal run.

## Questions
1. Tolerance: OK to start from relative 1e-3 (driven) / absolute 1e-9 (control) and tighten to measured*10 if the residual is far smaller?
2. Control "exactly": storage is only exact to solver convergence (~water_tolerance, default I use 1e-6 head tol). OK to use a 1e-9 content-integral tolerance, or should it be looser (e.g. 1e-8)?

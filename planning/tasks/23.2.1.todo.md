### Coincident holdout note
- The three centre triples are the reserved holdout configurations. Per the spec (`SOLUTE_VERIFICATION_SPEC.md` "Norms, refinement and budgets"), existing study outputs at a coincident holdout time do not constitute the reserved independent experiment. The centre rows are study outputs only; the holdout rows remain the independent test and are not run here.

# 20.2.2 — Full `SoluteConfig` mapping and zero-allocation compat step in `solute.rs`

## Goal
Extend `SoluteWorkspace` with preallocated Fortran compatibility buffers, map the full multi-material, per-species boundary, and kinetic sorption configuration into `SoluteStepConfig`/`SoluteStepState`, and eliminate all heap allocations during `advance_fortran_compatible_step` execution.

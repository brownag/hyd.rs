## Notes
- No existing `zbrak`/`zbrent`/`SolMass` port anywhere in `crates/`; they are ported here (no reuse available, nothing outside scope).
- `initialization.rs` does not exist yet; the spec's "mod.rs" is `solute_fortran_compat/mod.rs`.
- The adapter already maps `mass_initialization`/`equilibrium_initialization` switches and requires `initial_dissolved_concentration`; adapter, `types.rs` and `Cargo.*` are not touched, so the kernel takes its own `pub(crate)` primitive records and the 18.10.1 driver will map the state. The ordering of the `iDualPor` and `lEquil` override stays in the caller function.
- Source quirks to preserve and pin by tests: `xKs2` computed from `rKa1/rKd1`; the `Par(6)` temperature factor uses `TDep(jjj+7)`; `MassInit` uses `lLinear(NS)` and `ThOld/ThOldIm/ths`; `NonEqInit` uses `TempO`, not `TempN`; ZBRAK1 reports only the first sign-change bracket.
- Risk: the f32 `10**dx2` and `Conc**fExp` rounding may differ in the last ulp from gfortran's `powf`; tests compare with a tolerance and the independent script, with no parity claim.
- Not in scope: the reader, water/heat initialization, `MoistDepIn`, parity claims.

## Notes
- Precedents are single-step, algebraic: `vapor_closed_pair_balance.rs:189-197` and `vapor_free_drainage_balance.rs:189-198` define residual = |dStorage - netIn| with dStorage = sum of trapezoid-weight x (liquid + vapor) new minus old (`closed_pair:~170-185`), netIn = (r_bot + upper_flux) x dt. Tolerance: `tol = max(DRIVEN_REL_TOL * |netIn|, DRIVEN_ABS_FLOOR)` with `DRIVEN_REL_TOL = 1e-9`, `DRIVEN_ABS_FLOOR = 1e-13` (`closed_pair:30-31`, `free_drainage:31-32`); zero-flux control abs 1e-12 (`closed_pair:33`). `vapor_balance.rs` is the per-node interior check, bounds loaded from file (`load_bounds`, l.33); not used here.
- Those precedents use PrescribedFlux/Flux with a converged single step (`water_tolerance 1e-3`, no Picard coupling). The met run has Picard tolerance effects and two source-ordered passes; the 1e-9 relative residual may not hold. I will measure first.
- Fortran BALANCE.OUT shows WatBalT 1.05e-4 cm (0.014 %) at 24 h; that is cumulative, a different, coarser quantity. Evidence only.
- Bottom fixed head: `lower` mode is Head in the driver; its flux = total_face_fluxes[0] (positive up per solver convention; confirm sign in probe).

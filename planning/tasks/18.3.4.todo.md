## Notes
Scope is fixtures and task-local evidence only. Do not edit solver code, pinned source, existing fixtures, tolerances, or other fixture groups. Source contract identifies mobile-immobile coefficients at `SOLUTE.FOR:277–318,587–606,647–724` and dual-domain terms at `Coeff:277–318,647–724`; `MatSet` assembles resulting coefficients. Positive immobile water selects `lMobIm` absent bacteria; `iDualPor>0` is caller-selected and requires externally produced dual-water histories. `NEquil`, `SorbConc`, and `NonEqInit` calls are commented/inactive; initial pools/state must be explicit where applicable. Existing reactions fixtures exclude these modes and are not substitutes. No normalized-parity, whole-program parity, physical-validation, or inactive-helper claim is authorized.

**Source-mapping blocker:** `INPUT.FOR` reads `iDualPor` through the water-model selector: water model 6 maps to `iDualPor=1` and model 7 to `iDualPor=2` (`INPUT.FOR:611–625`). `HYDRUS.FOR` derives dual state with `InitW` (`215`) and `InitDualPor` (`234–235`), where histories use `ParD(7:9,M)`, water state, pressure head and material IDs (`INPUT.FOR:472–529`). During each time step, active `WATFLOW` computes exchange `SinkIm`, evolves `ThNewIm`, and adjusts pressure/state (`WATFLOW.FOR:1038–1071`); `Solute` consumes `ThOldIm`, `ThNewIm`, `SinkIm`, and dual-flow terms (`HYDRUS.FOR:506–517`; `SOLUTE.FOR:286–313,647–724`). These histories are produced by the out-of-scope water-flow solver, not read from `SELECTOR.IN`, `PROFILE.DAT`, or `LEVEL_01.DIR`. The fixture contract has no supported normalized-history-to-driver mapping or way to inject those arrays into `Solute`; deriving them independently would not make the active call path consume them. Therefore a source-authenticated dual-domain discriminator cannot be created under this task's permitted interfaces. Stop before any fixture or reference run; request an orchestration amendment that defines a supported external-history injection/mapping or scopes a driver harness.

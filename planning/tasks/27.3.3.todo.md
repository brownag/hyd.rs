## Notes
- Defect line: `water_solver.rs:316` `let mixed_head = 0.1 * head_temp + 0.9 * candidate_workspace.trial_heads[node];`. There is no `min(hSat)`.
- `source_h_sat` is at `water_solver.rs:202`. The material list is `context.source_materials[material]` (`.model.parameters()`, `.table`). The `saturated` vec is built just above the loop at `:306-310`.
- `evaluate_vapor_liquid` is at `:552`. It uses the table inside the table span, otherwise `model.evaluate`. A clamped head of `hSat` is at or near the table top, so the ConSat equivalence must be checked on both paths.
- Heads at or below `hSat` are bit-identical, since `min` is the identity. The dry uniform and heat-boundary cases (h = -1e4) should be unchanged.
- Regression scope: any vapor case with a saturated or ponded node changes behavior toward Fortran. An expectation that breaks is a fixture re-check, not a tolerance change.
- Out of scope: `kernels.rs`, the compat path, fixtures, criteria, tolerances, and the untracked `vapor_nonuniform_parity.rs` and `27.3.2.todo.md`.
- The baseline for `vapor_nonuniform_parity` shows 3 failed, not 1. Two of these are consequences of the same NaN (the off-case and the output-unavailable test). The expected after-fix result of 4 of 5 passing comes from the diagnosis.

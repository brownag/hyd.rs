## Notes
- criteria.json bounds are per FIELD (fields table, e.g. head abs 0.5 / rel 0.02), not per case; `vapor_fortran_parity.rs` looks up `crits[name]` for every case (FLUX_CASES, FIXEDHEAD_CASES). So no criteria edit is needed: the met case reuses the same field rules, and the existing hash pin guards them.
- Unit rule: ET mm/d from `calculate_bare_soil_et`; rSoil = EvapP*rConv/TTConv, rConv=0.001*xConv, TTConv=86400*tConv. Each record applies to the interval ending at its tAtm. ATMOSPH.IN has 11 columns; hCritA positive in input, stored negative; TopInf=t.
- `second_shift_would_flip` expected true on dry head-mode steps; not a failure.
- Tests must not read the untracked raw Fortran outputs; switch times come from RUN_INF.OUT facts, hard-coded with provenance comment.
- Fixture time points are 0..240 h step 24 h; the 120.1 and 42.7 switches fall between prints, so switch times are only verifiable via the driver trace.

## Discovered
- Met discrimination (vapor-fields-nonzero) entry for `vapor_met_bare_soil_on` needs a criteria edit; follow-up for user approval (D-9). Not added.
- `VaporWaterHeatDriver` has no atmospheric entry point, so the support driver re-composes its two source-ordered passes (private `heat_pass`, `VaporState::stage/accept` are public) around `advance_atmospheric_step_vapor`. A production met driver will need the same composition in `src/`.
- With lMeteo, `SetMeteo` receives `tTop` in its `TAver` slot, so the Dirichlet top temperature is (TMax+TMin)/2 per record. Not ported in `src/` (31.3.1 covers only EvapP); the test driver builds one `VaporConfig` per record. Without it the temperature rows fail (fixture top 27.0 at 24 h).
- Probe: an abrupt profile (top -1000, rest -100) is NonConverged at step 1; a smooth ramp to -1000 solves (finite, no singular pivot). Not a D-10 matter.

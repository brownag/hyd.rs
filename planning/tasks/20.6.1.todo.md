## Step 7 diagnosis, round 2 (2026-10-19, amendment scope)
- Earliest non-finite value: `old_source_g1` is all NaN for both fixtures (`boundary.rs` guard at the `old_source_g1` array, index 15 of the array list). Traced through `driver.rs` `coeff_level_into` (line ~1414, `g1[i] = terms.g1`) to `psi1 = NaN`.
- Cause: `biology.rs`/`driver.rs` take `iPsi1 = int(ChPar(jjj+14)) = GamL1 = 3`. `SMax1 = ChPar(jjj+18) = xMuS = 0`. With `iPsi=3` and `SMax=0`, `Blocking` gives `Binf = inf` and `psi = NaN`. That matches pinned `SOLUTE.FOR:1561-1594` arithmetic exactly. Then `g1 = g1 - ThW*(rKa1*psi1 + ...)` (`SOLUTE.FOR:710`) carries the NaN.
- Pinned Fortran reference output (`0004-blocking_type_3_noop/reference-run/run/*solute1.out`) is NaN in `cTop`/`cBot`/`cRoot` for every time row. The runner's NaN-to-NaN compare (`run_solute_parity.py:162`) would accept NaN matches.
- Experiment, temporary and reverted: disabled the two `endpoint primitives must be finite` checks in `boundary.rs`. Both fixtures then run to completion, and the NaN concentration columns match the Fortran pattern. But the Rust time column diverges at row 2 (Rust `0.2286`, Fortran `0.2000`). Fortran keeps `dt = 0.1` under NaN, while Rust shrinks `dt`. This comes from NaN-driven `dt_max` control in `driver.rs` (`dt_max.min(...)` at ~1461 versus gfortran NaN `MIN`/comparison semantics).
- Conclusion: a surgical fix is not possible within the allowed files. Matching needs (a) removing the ADR-0014 finiteness guard for NaN-valued primitives, and (b) reproducing gfortran NaN-driven time-step control. Both are design decisions beyond "surgical". Stopping per ground rules.
- Restored `boundary.rs` and `driver.rs` to `HEAD` (`git checkout --`). No Rust change is kept. Steps 3-4 were not rerun.

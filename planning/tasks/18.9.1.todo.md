## Notes
- Existing in `processes.rs`: `rmd`, `coeff_factors`, `prev_species_factors`, `old_level_factors` (the Gam/xMu values), `sorb_conc`. NEquil is in `nonequilibrium.rs`; Retard/Disper/PeCour in `transport.rs` (retard needs dConc and `fi`, which this task supplies as `IsothermFactors`; not touched). Retard itself is not duplicated.
- Not ported here (other owners): `Solute` loop/Iter/convergence/dt cut-back (18.10.1/18.11.1), boundary cv terms (18.5.x), MassTran (accounting), lBact/Blocking/Deposit (18.9.3/18.9.5), MatSet.
- Solute 44-230 species iteration is mostly driver; the only process-level semantics for this kernel are the `jS>1` coupling to the previous species (cPrev = Conc(jS-1) at new level, cPrevO at old) and per-species `jjj` offset, which come in through inputs.
- Everything is default REAL (f32), source expression order; `**` as `powf`, `alog` as `ln`. No new files besides this TODO; no mod.rs edit.
- Risk: `Level.eq.NLevel` vs old-level differences (cc, ss, SConcO only at final level) must be explicit flags.

# D-26.1 Diagnosis: vapor-coupled head drift

Scope: diagnosis only. No tracked src, test, fixture, criteria, or pinned Fortran changed. All instrumentation is a scratch copy under `.development/D-26.1/` (untracked, not committed). Base: `6959c0b` (fast-forwarded from `a155e06`).

## Baseline reproduced

Vapor driver, `vapor_heatbc_fd_on`, scratch harness (copy of `vapor_heat_boundary_parity.rs`), max abs head error vs fixture:

| t | max abs head error (cm) |
|---|---|
| 6 | 0.1196 |
| 24 | 0.5396 |
| 72 | 0.7330 |
| 240 | 5.7035 |

This matches the orchestrator numbers.

## Results by check

**Step 1, physics vs driver split (done, decisive).** Zeroing all vapor-only terms (vapor head conductivity, vapor thermal conductivity, vapor storage, and the vapor capacity correction) in the vapor driver gives head frozen at -10000 cm at every print time. Against `vapor_heatbc_fd_off` (vapor-off fixture) the max error is 9.1e-12 cm. The vapor driver therefore adds no head motion of its own: the deck has uniform initial head and head boundaries, so all head motion comes from vapor terms. The legacy-driver run on the fd_on deck was not possible (`VaporNotSupported` from `HeatWaterDriver`), so the legacy comparison used the existing fd_off control instead.

**Ablation (single vapor term removed, compared with fd_on fixture):**

| Variant | max abs error at t=240 (cm) |
|---|---|
| Full vapor physics (baseline) | 5.70 |
| Vapor head conductivity (ConVh) zeroed | 30.9 |
| Vapor thermal conductivity (ConVT) zeroed | 17.7 |
| Vapor storage and capacity correction zeroed | 1.97 |

Each vapor term moves head by about 1e4 cm. The 5.7 cm net drift is a cancellation of large terms. A small relative error in any one of them can produce cm-scale drift.

**H3, single precision (refuted as main cause).** Emulating the Fortran REAL*4 arithmetic of `ConVapor` and `VaporContent` in f32 (scratch `kernels.rs`, switch `D261_F32`) changes head by about 0.02 cm. The t=240 drift goes from 5.7035 to 5.7245 cm. Single-precision kernel rounding cannot explain 5.7 cm.

**H1, time level in pass two (current choice confirmed).** Pass two using the accepted (old) temperatures instead of the first heat result gives 789 cm error at t=240. The current Fortran-consistent choice (`HYDRUS.FOR:402-497`, `TempN` updated between passes) is the one that matches.

**Temperature sensitivity.** A uniform shift of the pass-two temperature estimate:

| Shift (C) | Error at t=24 (cm) | Error at t=240 (cm) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.54 | 5.70 |
| -0.001 | 0.62 | 6.57 |
| +0.001 | 1.40 | 5.41 |
| -0.005 | 4.12 | 10.0 |
| +0.005 | 4.84 | 9.66 |

Head is very sensitive to temperature (about 1e3 cm/C). Both signs of a uniform shift make the error worse, so the drift is not a uniform temperature offset. The Rust temperatures are 0.0048 to 0.0050 C above the fixture at all times, but the fixture is printed at f8.2 (0.01 C resolution), so this offset is at the output quantization limit and cannot be called a temperature error.

**Step 5, boundary rows (partial).** The largest head errors sit at interior nodes (indices 1 to 6). The end nodes are head-fixed. Boundary-row vapor terms were not printed, so the check is incomplete.

**Step 6, driver machinery (partial).** The accepted trace has 2401 entries, 2400 at dt=0.1 and one other entry (not identified). No retries are visible in the accepted trace. Per-step iteration counts are not exposed by the candidate, so Iter=2 was not verified.

**Not run.** Step 3 (commit-time vapor content: pre-solve vs post-solve) and step 7 (predictor swap).

## Ranking (updated)

1. **Vapor-term physics, conditioning amplified by large cancellation (primary).** The drift lives entirely in vapor-term-driven motion (step 1). Individual vapor terms are about 1e4 cm each, so a small relative mismatch in ConVT, ConVh, or the storage term gives the observed drift. Not yet localized to a specific term. Next check: scale each term by (1 +/- 1e-4) and look for the fingerprint that reproduces the drift pattern (t=6 0.12, t=24 0.54, t=72 0.73, t=240 5.70 cm).
2. **H1, time level (medium-low).** The current pass-two temperature is the right time level. A uniform temperature offset does not reduce drift, so this is not the cause unless the mismatch is spatially patterned. Step 3 (commit-time vapor content) still open.
3. **H3, single precision (refuted).** About 0.02 cm effect.
4. **H4, boundary-row vapor terms (low for head).** Head-fixed ends; still open for `v_liquid` at boundaries.

## Perturbation test (scale each term by 1 +/- 1e-4, fd_on)

Max abs head error (cm) at t=6 / 24 / 72 / 240; sign is sign of (candidate - fixture) at the worst node. Baseline: 0.1196 / 0.5396 / 0.7330 / 5.7035.

| Term (scratch switch) | x(1+1e-4) | x(1-1e-4) |
|---|---|---|
| ConVh (vapor head conductance) | 0.1253 / 0.2903 / 0.6245 / 6.2992 | 0.1138 / 0.9657 / 1.3961 / 5.1078 |
| ConVT (vapor thermal conductivity, matrix/flux) | 1.1861 / 1.1287 / 2.4425 / 5.6912 | 1.1059 / 0.5229 / 2.0349 / 6.9983 |
| Storage (ThetaV in water rhs) | 0.1218 / 0.5273 / 0.7193 / 5.7201 | 0.1173 / 0.5520 / 0.7467 / 5.6869 |
| Capacity correction (ThetaV term in Cap) | 0.1196 / 0.5396 / 0.7330 / 5.7035 | 0.1196 / 0.5396 / 0.7330 / 5.7035 |
| Water-row thermal term (ConVT in rhs only) | 0.7597 / 3.0707 / 5.2248 / 8.0853 | 0.8282 / 2.5525 / 5.4448 / 9.0270 |

Sign: ConVh, ConVT and the water-row thermal term change sign of the t=6 error between the two perturbations. Storage keeps the sign throughout.

Storage at +/-3% (scratch): t=6 1.230 / 4.731 / 7.667 / 10.05 cm (x0.97) and 1.298 / 4.212 / 8.170 / 10.68 cm (x1.03).

Conclusion: no single term reproduces the baseline shape with the baseline magnitude. ConVh, ConVT and the water-row thermal term are extremely sensitive (1e-4 relative gives 0.5 to 1 cm at t=6) but reshape the drift non-monotonically. Storage is the only term whose 1e-4 response keeps the baseline shape, but its effect is about 0.02 cm at t=240, and at 3% it gives roughly 10 cm with a wrong t=6 value. Capacity correction is negligible. The drift needs a combined or non-scalar error (for example, a small mismatch in the temperature field that feeds the thermal terms), not a single scaled term.

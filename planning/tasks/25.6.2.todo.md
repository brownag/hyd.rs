## Notes
Source equations (grouping as source; contract "Equations and variable ownership").
Interior node i (1..n-2), dxA=x_i-x_{i-1}, dxB=x_{i+1}-x_i, dx=(dxA+dxB)/2,
conA=ConL,face + (ConVh_{i-1}+ConVh_i)/2 (likewise B), thermal faces
CTA/CTB=(ConVT pair)/2, all `WATFLOW.FOR:235-267`, implemented in `water.rs`
`assemble_water_rows`.

Water discrete row actually solved (heads h^{k+1}):
  -conA/dxA*h_{i-1} + (conA/dxA+conB/dxB+C_eff*dx/dt)*h_i - conB/dxB*h_{i+1}
  = C_eff*dx/dt*h_i^{it} - (ThL_new-ThL_old)*dx/dt - (KL_A-KL_B)*g
    - sink*dx + CTB*(T_{i+1}-T_i)/dxB - CTA*(T_i-T_{i-1})/dxA
    - (ThV_new-ThV_old)*dx/dt
where h^{it} is the iterate at which coefficients were evaluated (pre-final-solve).
Residual form: R_i = lhs(h^{k+1}) - rhs. Equivalent flux form I will close:
  R_i/dx = [liquid+equiv-vapor storage rate] + [F_B - F_A]/dx + sink - T-term,
with storage = C_eff*(h_new-h_it)/dt + (ThL_new-ThL_old)/dt + (ThV_new-ThV_old)/dt,
phase face fluxes F_phase,B = -conB_phase*(h_{i+1}-h_i)/dxB (+ gravity, liquid
only) from `liquid_conductivities`/`vapor_head_conductivities`, vapor thermal
face flux from `vapor_thermal_conductivities` and `temperatures` (pass-two
Con* are the stored pass-two values). Storage coefficient: the source's
`C_eff*(dh)` is an implicit linearization of d(theta)/dh, NOT the continuum
change theta_new-theta_old; the diagnostic reports both
  S_src=C_eff*(h_new-h_it)/dt   and   S_cont=(Th_new-Th_old)/dt (+vapor)
and their difference (the lag), never asserts they agree. C_eff literal
conversion (`WATFLOW.FOR:958`): C_eff=(1-rho_vs*Hr/rho)*C_L + ThetaV*0.018015*9.81/(8.314*TK)*xConv;
the continuum conversion would divide by xConv (h_m=h/xConv); the independent
check A5 computes both and reports the ratio xConv^2 explicitly as a
discrepancy, asserting only the literal source form.

Heat: (`TEMPER.FOR` TempAdj 180-268; `adjust.rs`; `solve_vapor_heat_transport`)
g0 = -Lat*(vVGrad + (ThVNew-ThVOld)/dt), Lat=xLatent/(xConv*tConv^2) (`kernels::x_latent` is
a hand-reimplemented-independently reference in the test), vVGrad centered
with source factor 2 (`TEMPER.FOR:202,211`), Cv=1.8e6/(xConv*tConv^2).
Sensible contribution: Cv*(v_vapor_i * dT/dx) centered; latent contribution:
g0 as above per level (old/new). Heat residual per interior row:
  Cap_i*(Tn-To)/dt*... - [Cond-face divergence] - Cv*vapor advective - g0 = residual.

Report fields feeding each term: `liquid/vapor_contents_{old,new}` (storage);
`liquid/vapor/total_face_fluxes` and `liquid/vapor_velocities` (transport and
sign); `liquid_conductivities`, `vapor_head_conductivities`,
`vapor_thermal_conductivities` (row faces); `temperatures`, `passes[1].heads`
(solution), `timestep`; `heat_flux`, `passes[i].temperatures`,
`passes[0].temperature_estimate` (heat levels).

Interior vs Dirichlet: residuals only for nodes 1..n-2 are asserted. Boundary
faces (top/bottom) get a separate `ReservoirBookkeeping` struct: boundary face
flux and half-cell storage, reported "as computed from published fluxes", with
a doc note that `H/balance.rs`/`T/heat_balance.rs` reservoir terms are not a
closure and never asserted. No forced closure.

Bounds (approved 2026-11-12): residual <= 1e-9*max|row term| with 1e-15 absolute
floor, water and heat; two named constants `VAPOR_WATER_BALANCE_REL_TOL`,
`VAPOR_HEAT_BALANCE_REL_TOL` plus `VAPOR_BALANCE_ABS_FLOOR`, doc comment citing
"approved 2026-11-12, planning/STATE_25.md numeric gate; migrate to
reference/vapor_fortran/criteria.json `checks` at gate creation". Fresh-
coefficient closure: reported only.

Independent checks (`vapor_analytical.rs`), each with assumptions:
 A1 Material/kernel hand values: choose T=20C, theta, theta_s, h so that
    DiffT, Tau, rho_vs, Hr, ConVh, ConVT (1-K difference), ThetaV are computed
    in-test from the contract formulas with separate code (no call to product
    helpers). Bound: rel 1e-12 (~20 flops incl. exp/pow, f64 eps 2.2e-16 times
    ~50 amplification), predeclared. Assumes xConv,tConv>0, positive air, enhancement off/on by case.
 A2 Isothermal head-gradient limit: T uniform, vapor thermal term vanishes, row
    reduces to Richards plus ConVh; check row vs hand-computed tridiagonal on
    a seeded nonuniform mesh, abs-rel 1e-13. Assumes uniform T, h and Con fixed.
 A3 Zero-gradient: h linear with gradient -g so liquid face flux zero and uniform T: all vapor and thermal terms vanish; row RHS equals storage only.
 A4 Zero-vapor: ConVh=ConVT=0, ThV constant => rows equal liquid-only
    `assemble_water_rows` reduction computed by hand; and total flux equals liquid.
 A5 Capacity conversion discrepancy (literal xConv vs continuum), as above.
 A6 Heat: TempAdj-term hand reference on nonuniform mesh with a fixed vapor
    velocity profile, levels old/new, factor-2 gradient; Lat computed in test.
 No analytical solution for the coupled nonlinear case is claimed.

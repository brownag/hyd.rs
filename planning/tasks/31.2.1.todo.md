## Notes
- Fortran provenance: `lMeteo` is read in `INPUT.FOR:38`. `MeteoIn` (`INPUT.FOR:934-1066`) reads site metadata, radiation coefficients, measurement heights, and crop configuration.
- Error 33 gotcha: in `TIME.FOR:638-639, 663` and `HYDRUS.FOR:830`, `Aero` raises Error 33 if $0.667 \cdot h_{\text{crop}} \ge z_w$ or $z_t$. For `iCrop=0`, `CropRes` (`TIME.FOR:738`) assigns $h_{\text{crop}} = 0.1\,\text{cm}$, requiring measurement heights in cm ($z_w = 200\,\text{cm}, z_t = 190\,\text{cm}$).
- Deck structure: when `AtmBC=true` and `KodTop=-4`, Fortran skips reading the `rTop rBot rRoot` record in `SELECTOR.IN`. Both `ATMOSPH.IN` and `Meteo.in` are opened by `HYDRUS.FOR:180-188`.
- Vapor flag check in `run_vapor_reference.py`: line 264 currently tests `"f f f t f f" in selector`, which must be updated to also recognize `"f f t t f f"` when `lMeteo=true`.
- Constraints: never touch `criteria.json` (owned by 31.2.3). Keep planning doc updates in separate `docs(planning):` commits.

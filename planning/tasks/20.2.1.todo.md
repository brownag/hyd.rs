# 20.2.1 — Preallocated scratch buffers in `assembly.rs`, `driver.rs`, and `iteration.rs`
## Goal
Eliminate heap allocations in the solute transport inner loop (`solve_pass_with_scratch`, `solute_step_with_scratch`, and `solve_solute_with_nonlinear_with_scratch`) by introducing caller-provided slice assembly, a reusable `SoluteDriverScratch` buffer container, and in-place `SoluteStepState::copy_from` rollback.

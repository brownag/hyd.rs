The earliest material divergence is already in the S1 assembled equation: Rust's instrumented local reaction rate is `gamma=32.2 h^-1` and local diagonal/RHS at an interior node are `6.923` / `6.88`; Fortran's first species has `dt=0.03125`, `Conc=1`, `Sorb=0`, `R=-0.172000021`, `B=0`, `D=6.89433336`, `E=0.00358333392`, `F=6.85850000` for an interior node. The matrix structures and source/mass terms are not the same discretization; the Fortran trace includes finite-element mass terms while Rust uses local nodal mass derivatives. Do not interpret the raw coefficient difference as a direct unit-for-unit parity check.

The clearer reaction-chain divergence is S2. Rust forms an S2 source of `0.170931677018633543` at every node and predicts `0.0123452027313761`. Fortran's S2 source `R` is `0.170928374`, with nonzero neighbor couplings and a diagonal near `3.44716668` at interior nodes; its solved concentration is about `0.01242224`. Because S1 itself already diverges and the matrix formulations differ, these first-step traces do not yet isolate whether the remaining chain-rate difference is caused by reaction parameter mapping, discretization, or both.

`reaction_zero` is the inactive-process control. Its Rust and Fortran output agree at all nine nodes for S1 (`1`), S2 (`0.25`), and S3 (`0`) after one interval. The equality shows that this setup's transport/storage path can agree when the reaction chain is inactive, but does not by itself establish equality of reaction assembly. `reaction_distinct` confirms nonzero chain behavior: Rust uniform S2 is `0.0123173134954` and S3 is `7.912014844e-5`; Fortran's second species is about `0.01238366` and third about `5.15181e-5` at interior nodes. ## Findings and limits

- Supported: reaction activation is where this controlled setup first develops a meaningful cross-solver difference; the inactive control agrees.
- Supported: S1 already has a small first-step difference, and S2/S3 amplify the discrepancy. The earliest observed assembly difference is in the first-species matrix, before downstream chain sources are assembled.
- Not established: whether the primary cause is differing spatial mass discretization, reaction coefficient mapping, or an additional parameter/precision convention. The printed Fortran values are rounded, and the current trace does not normalize both formulations to a common weak-form equation.
- Follow-up should derive the Fortran and Rust first-species equations symbolically in common units, reconcile element-to-node mass weighting and boundary treatment, then compare the effective first-order sink coefficient before examining the S1→S2 and S2→S3 rates.

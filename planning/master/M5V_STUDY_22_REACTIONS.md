### Classifier bounds
`run_solute_convergence.py` accepts `--bounds-source <bounds.json> --bounds-review <review.json>` (together). The classification reports 1050 findings per group, all `inconclusive`: 654 with `arithmetic_bound_missing` and 396 with `arithmetic_bound_missing` plus `quadrature_bound_missing`. I did not produce a bounds source, per the orchestrator's instruction. What the classifier needs (`solute_convergence_evidence.py:254-337`):
- `bounds.json`: schema `solute-convergence-bounds-v1` with keys `schema_version`, `cases_sha256` (equal to the casebook SHA), `producer_sha256` (equal to the batch's producer map) and `bounds_by_launch`; each launch maps to a list of entries with exactly `species`, `observable` (one of `concentration.{l1,l2,linf,endpoint_linf,interior_linf}`, `inventory.*`, `reaction.production|loss`, `boundary.instantaneous.0|1`), `time` (a scheduled time; concentration entries only at 0 or the analysis times), `arithmetic`, `coordinate`, `quadrature` (each a finite nonnegative number or null) and non-empty `evidence`. No duplicate (species, observable, time) channel.
- `review.json`: schema `solute-convergence-bounds-review-v1` with `cases_sha256`, `producer_sha256`, `bounds_path`, `bounds_sha256`, a non-empty `authorization` string, and `evidence_sha256` mapping at least one derivation file to its SHA-256.
- Authority: the `authorization` and the derivation evidence must come from a root review by the orchestrator; a worker may not self-author them (the existing unit test uses a mocked review). The producer map is baked into the batch, so the bounds must be written against these exact tool hashes. The arithmetic bound would be the f32 accumulation bound for the step count, which is a derivation, not a solver output.

## Floors and uncertainty
- Reference uncertainty (uniformization tail and roundoff) is 1e-16 to 4e-16 in the comparisons, far below every reported error.
- The f32 floor (about 1e-7 relative) is not reached: the smallest resolved error is about 3e-4 (S3), and no plateau or sign change appears in the S1 or S2 profile errors across the three levels. Signed inventory and reaction errors for S2/S3 change sign between times (see the table), which is the expected chain transient, not a refinement artifact.
- `reaction_zero`: solver and reference identical (zero errors, `zero` branch, `temporal_l2` = 0 for all species).

## Observations against the spec's expected behavior
- Closed reaction `m' = Bm` with a parent-only start: S1 decays, S2 and S3 grow from zero and match the reference to first order in dt, in both `reaction_distinct` (distinct rates, terminal loss, unequal storage) and `reaction_equal` (equal first two rates).
- `reaction_zero` retains the initial state exactly.
- S1 is identical between `reaction_distinct` and `reaction_equal` (same parent rate), as it must be.
- Largest relative errors are in the youngest daughter S3 at t = 1 (1.75e-2 at dt 0.03125 for `reaction_distinct`, 2.32e-2 for `reaction_equal`; 6.7e-2 at dt 0.125) because its reference value is small there.
- No structural anomaly (constant offset or stalled convergence) was observed.

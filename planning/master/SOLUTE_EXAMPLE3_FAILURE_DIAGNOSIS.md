## Finding and evidence gap

Supported: the two task-local runs start from identical zero concentration arrays, use the same authored physical and species fields, reach the shared 50 h printed checkpoint, and produce a large mixed-sign concentration disagreement. Printed Fortran water fields support the intended saturated θ and flux magnitude. Rust's first interval assembly is captured at inlet/midpoint/outlet for each species.

Not established: the Fortran first-step assembly and solved concentrations are absent (`TRACE15_3_FORTRAN_ROW=0`, `TRACE15_3_FORTRAN_SOLVED=0`). Consequently this run cannot identify the earliest cross-solver operator divergence or prove runtime dispersion, reaction-source, and boundary-row equality. Source differences such as Fortran's epsilon/upstream-weighted mass-lumped finite-element assembly and Rust's face-flux/control-volume assembly are candidate differences only; neither was isolated as causal here. The run is a finite 250 cm derived fixture, not a recovered manual deck or an analytical golden reference.

The smallest next discriminator is an amended, separately bounded Fortran-only first-interval trace with instrumentation triggered by the actual `t` value at the `Solute` call and limited to the same three representative physical locations/species. This orchestration consumed both approved 15.3.1 solver attempts, so no such run was performed. The unresolved 15.2.1 Fortran pre-assembly input-path blocker remains separate and untouched; its user-selected no-retry limit was honored.

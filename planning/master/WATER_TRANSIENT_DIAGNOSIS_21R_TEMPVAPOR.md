| 1141 | `solute1.out` row 1141, t = 114.1 | `Sum(cvTop)` = `CumCh(1,1)` | 49.0625 (`42444000`) | 49.0625 (`42444000`) | `0.49062E+02` / `0.49063E+02` | `OUTPUT.FOR:209` (`e13.5` write, libgfortran rounding) | `output_compat.rs:20-48` (`format_fortran_e`) |

`49.0625` is an exact binary tie at 5 significant digits:
- gfortran rounds exact ties to even. A probe (`fmt_probe/probe.f`) prints `0.125 → 0.1E+00`, `0.375 → 0.4E+00` and `49.0625 → 0.49062E+02`.
- `format_fortran_e` computes `(man_val * 1e5).round()` in f64 (`49062.5`), and `f64::round` rounds half away from zero, giving `49063`.

**Verdict:** hypothesis (b) holds (format tie-rounding). Hypothesis (a), f32 vs f64 accumulation, is refuted: Rust already accumulates `CumCh` in f32 at `compat_runner.rs:1294`, and the bits match. Hypothesis (c), solver drift, is refuted: `dt`, `cvTop` and `CumCh` agree bit for bit at every level, and every other record passes.

## Root-cause statement

**`temperature_coefficients`.** *Established:* the fixture fails only because of a decimal-formatting tie.
- The single-precision accumulator `CumCh(1,1)` holds exactly 49.0625 at `TLevel` 1141 in both programs (`42444000`).
- libgfortran prints exact ties rounded to even (`0.49062E+02`, confirmed by a standalone probe).
- Rust's `format_fortran_e` rounds the f64 product `49062.5` half away from zero (`0.49063E+02`).
- The water and solute states agree. Every other record passes, and `dt`, `cvTop` and `CumCh` are bit-identical for all 2000 levels.
- Which of (a), (b), (c) holds: (b).
- With the formatter prototype the fixture passes.

**`vapor_enabled_external_state`.** *Established:* the first divergence is `ConVT` at the first `SetMat` call.
- The pinned `Init` sets `iEnhanc = 1` unconditionally (`INPUT.FOR:1718`; the deck has no input for it). `ConVapor` then multiplies `ConVT` by the enhancement factor `eta` (`WATFLOW.FOR:911-916`).
- The Rust runner passes `i_enhanc = 0` at both `set_mat` call sites (`compat_runner.rs:180,1015`). This is the same class of defect as `lExtrap` and `lMinStep` in 21.3.1.
- The wrong `ConVT` changes `vTop` and the head solution through the thermal-vapor flux terms (`WATFLOW.FOR:224-226,257-262,405`), and through them `vNew(N)` and `cvTop`.
- With `i_enhanc = 1`, the full 65,880-row water trace agrees, and every `solute1/2/3.out`, `PROFILE.OUT` and `NOD_INF_V.OUT` conductivity record passes.

*Established*, two output-only defects in `NOD_INF_V.OUT`, masked at HEAD:
- `vTotal` is the REAL sum `vi + vVi` in Fortran (`OUTPUT.FOR:816`); Rust sums in f64 (`compat_runner.rs:468`).
- `vVapIso`/`vVapTerm` are computed in Fortran (`OUTPUT.FOR:769-772,783-786,804-810`) but hard-coded to 0 in Rust (`compat_runner.rs:479-480`).

With all three corrections the fixture passes.

*Refuted:* the SD-003 `hTemp`/`vCorr` alias with nonzero `vCorr` (`vCorr ≡ 0`; traces identical). Also refuted: `Temper` and temperature transport (unreachable).

## Suspected source defects

None. `iEnhanc = 1` is a deliberate default ("Enhancement factor for vapor flow, =1 OK", `INPUT.FOR:1710`). Round-half-even of exact binary ties is the libgfortran default (`ROUND=` unspecified), not a source defect. The REAL `vi+vVi` sum is ordinary single-precision output.

Cross-site check:
- `ConVapor` is the only consumer of `iEnhanc` (`WATFLOW.FOR:912`).
- Both `SetMat` call paths receive it (`WATFLOW.FOR:69`, `HYDRUS.FOR:229`).
- In Rust, both `set_mat` call sites hard-code 0.

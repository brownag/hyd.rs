## 2. Ranked mismatches with evidence

Ranking is by evidence strength and plausibility of a head effect at t=240. Items marked "refuted" were checked against both sides and found equivalent.

### Ranked list

1. **M3a (new, leading): Fortran head state is REAL*4, Rust is f64.**
   - Fortran: `WATFLOW.FOR:21` declares `double precision P,R,S,PB,RB,SB,PT,RT,ST,rMin,t,tOld` and does not include `hNew`, `hOld`, `hTemp`, `TempN`, `ConVT`, `ConVh`, `vVNew`, or `ThVNew`. `HYDRUS.FOR:65-67` double list covers only time variables and P/R/S/Q. No `implicit real*8` appears in `HYDRUS.FOR`, `WATFLOW.FOR`, or `TEMPER.FOR` (only `HYSTER.FOR` has one). The build flags in `reference/fortran_reference/PATCH_NOTES.md:5` are `-std=legacy -ffixed-line-length-none`, with no default-real-8 flag. So `hNew` is a default REAL (single precision) array.
   - Rust: `VaporState` heads are `Vec<f64>` (`heat/vapor/state.rs`, `candidate.heads`), and `trial_heads` is f64 (`water_solver.rs` ~296-310).
   - Consumer: every head difference in Veloc and ConVh/Hr (`WATFLOW.FOR:756-772`, `:903-905`), and the solved heads that feed the next iteration.
   - Expected direction at t=240: not determined by reading. Rounding of h near -10000 cm is ulp 2^-10 cm (about 1e-3 cm; worst-case error 4.9e-4 cm), and the fixture head differences are small, so the relative error of the head differences can be large. Node-varying.
   - Evidence needed: an untested rounding emulation. The H3 check only rounded kernel outputs (ConVapor, VaporContent) to f32, not the head state.

2. **M3b: Fortran temperature state is REAL*4 (`TEMPER.FOR:55`), Rust is f64.**
   - Fortran: `TEMPER.FOR:55` `TempN(i)=sngl(F(i))`; `TempN` and `TempO` are REAL (no double declaration in `TEMPER.FOR:12` or `:82`; `:12` double list is `B,D,E,F,t`). Level-2 latent at `TEMPER.FOR:213` uses `xLatent` declared real (`:310`), and `Lat` is `real Lat` (`TEMPER.FOR:182`).
   - Rust: `heat1`, `heat2`, `temperatures` are f64 (`driver.rs:460`, `:506-507`, `:216`).
   - Magnitude: rounding of about 1e-6 C at 20 C, times the diagnosis sensitivity (about 1e3 cm/C, `D-26.1.diagnosis.md`, temperature table), gives about 1e-3 cm. Node-varying. Expected to be small, but the vapor cancellation makes this non-negligible at the 1e-3 cm level.

3. **M3c: Fortran vapor arrays REAL (`ConVT`, `ConVh`, `vVNew`, `ThVNew`), Rust f64.**
   - Fortran: `WATFLOW.FOR:12-14`, `HYDRUS.FOR:91-93` list these as default REAL.
   - Rust: f64 throughout (`kernels.rs:9-92`, `water.rs` phase velocities).
   - Partially tested already: the diagnosis H3 rounded ConVapor and VaporContent outputs to f32 and moved head by 0.02 cm, so kernel-output rounding is refuted. Not yet tested: rounding of the stored velocities `vVNew` (which feed TempAdj) and the stored `ThVNew`. Expected small, low ranking.

4. **M5 (new, low): TempAdj latent and Cv are REAL.**
   - Fortran: `TEMPER.FOR:182` `real Lat`, `:310` real `xLatent`; `Cv` is implicit REAL in `TempAdj` (`TEMPER.FOR:191`, no declaration).
   - Rust: f64 (`adjust.rs:209-236`, `x_latent` at `adjust.rs` and `kernels.rs:94-108`).
   - Magnitude: relative 6e-8 on g0 = -Lat*(vVGrad+ThVGrad). Small. Low.

5. **M2: boundary-row vapor terms (low for head).**
   - Fortran: `WATFLOW.FOR:211-228` (ConB, ConTB at node 1), `:245-291` (PB/PT with storage). The fixture is Dirichlet at both ends (`SELECTOR.IN`: KodTop=1, KodBot=1).

## 3. Spatial classification

Interior node drift (indices 1 to 6) with end nodes head-fixed is the diagnosed pattern. A spatially patterned error must vary node to node.

- Explained by a node-varying error: M3a (head rounding of differences, node-varying), M3b (temperature rounding, node-varying but small), M3c (velocity and storage rounding, node-varying but small), M5 (latent, uniform in the sense of a constant multiplier but varies through T_i, so node-varying but tiny).
- Not explained by a spatially patterned error: uniform temperature offsets (already shown to worsen both signs, diagnosis table); scalar kernel rounding (H3, 0.02 cm); end-row boundary terms (M2, head-fixed ends); initial-state mismatch (M4, refuted); interior velocity storage (M1, refuted).
- Ambiguity: M3a could be node-varying and large enough. Its magnitude depends on how sensitive the vapor fluxes are to head differences, which is not measured here.
